Welcome to my website

I am an Assistant Professor in the Competence Centre for Sustainability at the University of Bozen-Bolzano and an affiliate of the Cognitive and Experimental Economics Laboratory (CEEL) at the University of Trento. Before joining the University of Bozen-Bolzano, I held research positions at the Universities of Trento and Pisa.


My interests include Behavioural and Experimental Economics and Game Theory, with a focus on pro-social and pro-environmental behaviour.


Pronouns: he/him.
