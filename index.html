Hey there! Welcome to my website 👋

I am an Assistant Professor in the Competence Centre for Sustainability at the University of Bozen-Bolzano and affiliated with the Cognitive and Experimental Economics Laboratory (CEEL) at the University of Trento. Before joining the University of Bozen-Bolzano, I held research positions at the Universities of Trento and Pisa.

My research interests include Behavioural and Experimental Economics and Game Theory, with a focus on social norms and cooperation.
